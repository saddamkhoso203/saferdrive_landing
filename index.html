FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

How do I turn Safer Drive on?

You check in according to your duty hours. Once you're checked in, Safer Drive is ready, and it locks your apps as soon as you start driving.

When are my apps locked?

As soon as your speed reaches 10 km/h, Safer Drive locks all your social media and messaging apps.

What happens when I stop?

If you're not moving, Safer Drive checks for two minutes and then unlocks your apps.

Will people know I'm driving?

Yes. With auto-reply on, people who message you get an automatic reply saying you're driving and will get back to them soon.

Is it only for company employees?

Safer Drive is designed for company employees who drive for work, but individuals can use it too, with a personal subscription.

What can companies see?

Companies registered with Safer Drive get an admin dashboard showing their employees' phone usage, check-in times, trips and blocked apps. They can also send custom notifications to their teams.

How much does it cost?

Safer Drive is free to download and free to use for 14 days. After that, you choose a monthly or yearly subscription. There are plans for both individuals and companies. For company plans, email info@saferdrive.app.

Is Safer Drive available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download Safer Drive from the App Store for iPhone or from Google Play for Android.
